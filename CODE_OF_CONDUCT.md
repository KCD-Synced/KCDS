# Code of conduct

KCDS is built by people who give their time to it. This code applies to everyone in the project's
spaces: this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, the documentation repository and
the KCDS Discord.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, with newcomers above all. Everyone was new once.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person, and say how it could be better.
- Stay on topic: issues are for bugs and requests, pull requests for changes.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on what goes into KCDS, even when you disagree with it.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Sharing another person's private information without their consent.
- Posting cheats, pirated copies of the game, or links to them.
- Spam, advertising, and repeated off-topic posts.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers: use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or issue, or
write to a moderator on the [KCDS Discord](https://discord.gg/Wte4rRJnSP). Reports are handled
privately. Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens

The maintainers decide what to do about a report. That can be a warning, removing content, or a
temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.
