# Credits

The board and pieces in `stream/objects/kcds/chess` are Poly Haven's
[Chess Set](https://polyhaven.com/a/chess_set) by Riley Queen, released under CC0 (public domain).
They were converted from its 1k glTF with `Dev/tools/assets/gltf/kcds_gltf.py`: one model per piece
and colour and the board, each at its own origin, and one 512-pixel texture pair per material.

The table (`table_fancy_a_square_small`) is the game's own prop, and the benches
(`Bench/bench_1place_low`) are the game's own seats: players sit on them with the game's own sit-down.
